**1. Improved AppTime email processing reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency**

Completed the objective to optimize and enhance the AppTime email processing system for high-volume event processing, with a target capacity of over 15 million events. Addressed recurring production and functional issues, including email notifications flooding for seal 85103, failures affecting high-volume seals such as 21090, a non-functional email switch API, and the accumulation of email records older than 90 days. Fixed the email switch API, optimized the email processing flow to handle varying event volumes more generically, improved the old-data deletion flow, and incorporated requested email header and body changes. These improvements addressed key functional and operational bottlenecks and strengthened the system's ability to support high-volume processing.

**2. Advanced CertGuardian Neo toward intelligent certificate observability and advisory**

Progressed CertGuardian Neo as a unified certificate observability solution bringing server-side and client-side certificate information together in a single dashboard, with an AI assistant intended to provide contextual knowledge and actionable insights. Developed CertGuardian developer skills to make certificate-related capabilities accessible within the IDE, enabling engineers to benefit from these capabilities earlier in the development lifecycle. Investigated and integrated Cryptolite with AppTime Service and CertGuardian Neo to identify relevant opportunities for cryptographic visibility and advisory. Also resolved LLM Suite API onboarding for CertGuardian Neo.

This work establishes a foundation for more proactive certificate risk identification, reduced manual analysis, and better-informed remediation. The next phase is to complete productionization, validate the advisory capabilities, and measure progress against the objective's targets for manual-effort reduction, proactive expiry-risk identification, and certificate-issue response time.

Progressed Photon upgrades from version 3.4.1 to 3.5.12, then to 3.5.15, and subsequently to 4.1.0. Executed these upgrades using Photon upgrade skills rather than relying on a purely manual approach. The initial upgrade from 3.4.1 to 3.5.12 was completed in one day. The subsequent progression demonstrates the application of a repeatable, skill-based approach to platform modernization. I am continuing the upgrade work toward Photon 4.1.1.

This approach promotes consistency, reuse, and a more repeatable upgrade process while applying AI-assisted engineering to practical technology lifecycle management.
