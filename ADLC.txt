---

4. Agentic SDLC Was the Preferred Starting Point

The screenshots show the proposed pipeline.

Phase 1. Synthesis

Purpose: Transform business requirements into structured, approved Epics.

Activities:

Requirement ingestion

AI-assisted synthesis

Socratic Q&A

Confidence gate

Requirement Knowledge Base

Epic decomposition

Product approval


Output: Approved Epics ready for grooming.


---

Phase 2. Grooming

Purpose: Convert approved Epics into execution-ready stories.

Activities:

Jira input

Context loading

Clarity scoring

Stakeholder reviews (QUAD)

Product review

Architecture review

Data review

Engineering review

Consistency checks


Output: Implementation-ready stories.


---

Phase 3. Execution

Purpose: Develop and validate the solution.

Activities:

Pre-flight readiness

Story implementation

Validation

Green log verification

Acceptance Criteria validation

Human Git operations

Pull Request


Output: Production-ready implementation.
