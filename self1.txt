1. What you achieved

In 2026, I focused on delivering reliable and secure technology outcomes, modernizing existing platforms, applying AI to improve engineering productivity, and progressing business-focused initiatives with potential for broader organizational impact. **1. Advanced the Branch Health Dashboard and established the foundation for an integrated operational view**

Initiated and progressed the Branch Health Dashboard, a business-focused initiative intended to provide branch managers with a consolidated view of branch health and operational priorities. Developed the initial UI using AI-assisted engineering and aligned it with JPMorganChase-approved MDS styling. The experience covers key capabilities including Required Actions, Control Exceptions, Today's Meetings, Tech Readiness, Performance at a Glance, Nearby Branches/ATMs, and Notifications.

Beyond UI development, worked through the architectural and data-integration considerations needed to progress toward a dependable, real-data-driven solution. This included assessing source-system integration approaches, operational versus analytical data requirements, data freshness, retention, and the proposed separation of near-real-time operational data from historical analytics. Also explored API and data dependencies across relevant source systems, including ARM/Kafka, Cortex, BDIS, and Snowflake.

Considered the operational requirements for timely status visibility and the user experience for identifying urgent, upcoming, and outstanding actions. The design approach aims to provide a consolidated overview while preserving source-system ownership of detailed information and actions.

This work established the initial user-facing foundation and clarified important technical dependencies for moving from a UI backed by mock data toward an integrated MVP. The next phase is to finalize priority data contracts, resolve dependencies with source-system owners, and validate the solution using real data.

**2. Improved AppTime email processing reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency**

Completed the objective to optimize and enhance the AppTime email processing system for high-volume event processing, targeting support for more than 15 million events. Addressed recurring functional and operational issues, including notification flooding for seal 85103, failures affecting high-volume seals such as 21090, a non-functional email switch API, and the accumulation of email records older than 90 days.

Fixed the email switch API, optimized the email processing flow to handle varying event volumes more generically, corrected the old-data deletion flow, and incorporated requested email header and body changes. These improvements addressed key processing bottlenecks and strengthened the system's ability to support high-volume processing and reliable email operations.

Investigated and integrated Cryptolite with AppTime Service and CertGuardian Neo to identify relevant opportunities for cryptographic visibility and advisory. Also resolved LLM Suite API onboarding for CertGuardian Neo.

This work establishes a foundation for more proactive certificate-risk identification, reduced manual analysis, and better-informed remediation. Productionization and validation of the advisory capabilities remain important next steps, with outcomes to be measured against the defined objective targets.
